## 0. Intellectual honesty first

**Category error is the main failure mode.** Bellman’s Principle of Optimality is a
necessity condition on optimal policies that licenses a functional equation. Krishna’s
counsel is an ethical–metaphysical pedagogy under a doctrine of Self, duty, and grace.
Treating rhyme as isomorphism produces cargo-cult Sanskrit *and* cargo-cult DP.

**What this note will not claim:**

- That the Gita “proves” MDPs, options, or AlphaZero.
- That search is yoga, or that \(\Phi\) is *tyaga*.
- That devotion is a learning algorithm.

**What it will claim:**

- A short list of **universal (or near-universal) truths** about agency that the poem
  presses with unusual force — defendable without Hindu metaphysics.
- A few **structural rhymes** with the math, marked Keep / Transform / Kill.
- A **revised, narrower thesis** for mario_ai after adversarial attack.
- One residue that is *about the experimenter*, not about the emulator.

Arnold’s Victorian preface (Christian parallelomania, “gift to England,” late dating)
already mediates the English. We read *this* poem, not raw Sanskrit.

## 1. The drama in one page

Arjuna stands between armies, sees teachers and kin he must kill, and **collapses** —
bow slips, body fails, he refuses to fight (Ch. I). Krishna answers across eighteen
chapters by braiding:

| Strand | Function |
|---|---|
| Knowledge of Self / field vs knower | dissolves confused authorship of death |
| Duty (*svadharma*) | refusal can be self-deception; own imperfect work > alien excellence |
| Renunciation of *fruit*, not of *act* | do the deed; drop possessive claim on outcomes |
| Equanimity | train affective weather so hard choice is possible |
| Discipline / habit | heart harder than wind, but restrainable by practice |
| Devotion / refuge | the poem’s own final solvent — not fully secularizable |

Closing (Ch. XVIII): “Meditate! And—as thou wilt—then act!” **and** “Fly to Me alone…
Be of good cheer!” Arjuna: “According to Thy word, so will I do!”

The cure is **not ethics alone**. Secular residue is rich; Arjuna’s own cited solvent
includes grace. Keep that distinction.

## 2. Universal truths extracted (spiritual or not)

These are claims a careful thinker can defend or reject **without** accepting rebirth,
avatar cycles, or personal deity. They are the poem’s pressure, not our endorsement.

### 2.1 Agency under conflict

1. **Moral injury can paralyze competent agents.** Knowing the “correct” role does not
   prevent somatic collapse when the role requires harming what one loves (I).
2. **You cannot exit action while embodied.** Sitting idle while craving is hypocrisy;
   thought is already a form of act (III). Renouncing *motion* ≠ renouncing *bondage*.
3. **Better fail at your fitting task than succeed at an alien one that “seems good”**
   (III, XVIII) — a claim about specialization and integrity under uncertainty.
4. **Abstaining from prescribed hard work from aversion or confusion is still a choice
   with a character** (XVIII: Tamasic / Rajasic false renunciation).

### 2.2 Fruit, proxies, and motive

5. **Outcome-contingent virtue is unstable.** Making the worth of an act depend on
   victory, spoils, status, or “heavenly” credit weakens fixation of mind (II).
6. **Intrinsic standard:** “Find full reward / Of doing right in right! Let right deeds
   be / Thy motive, not the fruit which comes from them” (II).
7. **The same outer deed is morally different under different motives** — duty vs
   gain/fame vs cruelty/negligence (XVII). Intention grades action.
8. **Small sincere acts outweigh empty spectacle** — leaf/flower/water with love vs
   lavish ritual without it (IX).

### 2.3 Desire, attention, character

9. **Affective capture chain:** sense-object → attraction → desire → passion →
   recklessness → loss of memory/purpose → ruin of agency (II).
10. **Desire (as craving) is anti-epistemic** — foils wisdom like smoke on fire (III).
11. **Like/Dislike bewilder judgment** about what is real or worth pursuing (VII).
12. **Character is patterned** (gunas secularized): clarity/steadiness vs
    restlessness/acquisitiveness vs dullness/collapse (XIV, XVII).
13. **Faith conforms to what the person already is** (XVII) — outlook and character
    co-evolve.
14. **Three reliable ruin doors:** lust, wrath, greed (XVI).

### 2.4 Training and knowledge

15. **Equanimity is a trainable stance**, not a mood — treat success/failure,
    praise/blame with less volatility so hard duty remains doable (II, VI, XII).
16. **Attention is hard but improvable by habit** — “Yet may it grow restrained by
    habit… By wont of self-command” (VI). Extremes of mortification undermine
    discipline (VI).
17. **Partial sincere progress is not nullity** (VI) — unfinished right orientation
    still shapes the agent (secularly: skill/character residual).
18. **Seeing “I am not the sole author”** (body, tools, effort, circumstance — and in
    the poem, more) can reduce grandiosity and neurotic guilt — **and** risks a moral
    blank check if abused (XVIII). Handle with care.
19. **Sense-driven living rebinds itself** (inverted tree, XV): deeds grow roots that
    make the next compulsive act harder to refuse. Detachment = cutting feedback loops.
20. **Multiple disciplines can work** (works / knowledge / devotion / hearing teachers)
    — but the poem still ranks and still commands concrete action (“fight”).

### 2.5 Explicitly non-secular (do not strip)

Immortal Self; avatar restoration of dharma; Krishna as ontological source and Time;
Swarga/Naraka accounting; “Fly to Me alone” superseding rites; grace that dwells in
devotees. Lossy if forced into algorithms.

## 3. How the academic authors think (re-embodied)

Before connecting, restate the *process* of the theory authors — not slogans.

### Bellman (1957)

- Start from **multi-stage decision**: state \(p\), choice \(q\), map \(T_q\).
- Reject enumerative \(MN\)-dim maximization (**curse of dimensionality**).
- **Imbed** one problem in a family; seek **policy structure** \(q^*(p)\).
- Transliterate the Principle of Optimality into a **functional equation**.
- Prefer **approximation in policy space** (guess \(q_0\), evaluate, improve) because
  applications suggest policies and yields monotone improvement when structure allows.
- Existence/uniqueness via contraction / survival lemmas — know when the equation is
  well-posed.

### Sutton & Barto

- RL is the *problem* of sequential decision-making, not “neural nets.”
- Reward communicates **what**, not **how** — shaping can create wrong optima.
- With a model, **planning and learning share backups**; sample model (simulator) is
  first-class.
- Knowing \(p\) does not dissolve computation: \(|S|\) still forces focused methods.

### Bertsekas

- Exact DP blows up; use **approximation in value space** / rollout.
- On-line lookahead ≈ **Newton step** on Bellman; off-line net supplies \(\tilde J\),
  not the solution.
- Major determinant of quality is often the **on-line** improvement step.
- Region of stability: bad \(\tilde J\) / base policy can yield infinite cost.

### Options / ExIt / AlphaZero / Go-Explore / DAgger / Ng

- Temporally extended actions induce SMDPs (SPS); indiscriminate macros can hurt.
- Search teaches; nets generalize and guide; remove expert improvement → DAgger.
- Perfect rules inside the tree; don’t learn \(f\) when \(f\) is free.
- First return, then explore — when progress-greedy detaches.
- IL regret scales with cost-to-go gap \(u\); absorbing deaths make \(u\) large.
- Potential shaping preserves optimal policies; non-potential proxies farm forever.

**Shared cognitive move across these authors:** name the **assumptions** (is \(f\)
known? deterministic? what is the true criterion?), reduce the problem by
**structure** (imbedding, options, potentials, archives), and refuse to confuse a
**proxy** with the terminal claim.

**Genuine keeps: ~1.5.** Poetry is allowed for motivation; lethal as claimed math.

### What Bellman/Sutton/Bertsekas would reject

Category error (metaphor as proof); anti-instrumentalism (“renounce fruit” as the
agent’s objective); authority substitution (“Krishna said → architecture”); vague
pluralism that erases planning priority when \(f\) is free.

### What a serious Gita commentator would reject

Instrumentalizing moksha into node counts; emptying *svadharma* into RAM
preconditions; confusing gunas/Self with \(s'=f(s,a)\); cosmetic Sanskrit while still
maximizing `beat_game` without acknowledging the jump.

### Unique residue the Gita adds (non-operational)

Almost nothing the math lacks for *building the stack*.

One agent-facing diagnosis, stated with unusual unity:

> **Fruit-attachment as a failure mode of the experimenter.**  
> The textbooks tell you how to optimize a criterion and when proxies preserve optima.
> They do not, as a single moral psychology, diagnose the temptation to treat
> intermediate metrics (val-acc, node reduction, “bench green”) as identity-fruit while
> drifting from the terminal claim (replay `beat=True`, honest knowledge tier, ROM-complete
> costs). The Gita binds paralysis, proxy-piety, and craving for heavenly fruit as one
> family — and insists that **false renunciation** (idle craving, or dropping hard
> prescribed work because it is unpleasant) is not liberation.

That is **discipline under ambition**, not a new backup operator. It belongs in
`notes/` as research ethos, not in `mario/search.py`.

## 5. Adversarial attack on our own theory thesis

Original thesis (compressed):

> Deterministic resettable emulator ⇒ two-tier Option-SMDP; search produces verified
> options; meta-search finds whistle foresight myopic planners miss; nets serve search.

Devil’s-advocate scorecard:

| Attack | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Search primary = selection bias (human/Codex routes; 6-2 open) | **Wounded** — survives for stock Mario under route-aware search, not as universal law |
| Bellman/Newton ⇒ beam+topk | **Wounded hard** — motivates model-based lookahead; does **not** prove beam; topk can break improvement |
| Options = named macros, not \(\langle\mathcal{I},\pi,\beta\rangle\) | **Wounded** — scaffold yes; SPS-complete no |
| Whistle “meta-intelligence” = incomplete greedy model by construction | **Wounded** — real opacity gap; marketing inflated |
| IL ceiling vs Foster/Song | **Wounded** — “wrong main line given \(f\)” survives; “~0.45 destiny” does not |
| Is \(f\) really known? (pipe FF, SMA4 desync, injections) | **Wounded** — known under adapter contract |
| Soft falsifiability | **Survive if hardened** |
| Honesty gap (symbolic costs, regrants, STATUS vs prose) | **Biggest wound** |

### Revised maximally defensible thesis

For stock Mario under a **deterministic, snapshot-resettable emulator contract**, the
reliable closed-loop solver is **model-based search** (beam / coverage / Go-Explore)
over savestates, optionally accelerated by learned priors/values. When the game has
**long-horizon consumables and gated overworld structure**, wrap **replay-verified
temporally extended executors** (precondition + π + success termination) into a library
and plan with **non-myopic, execute-to-observe search**; myopic progress-greedy
baselines can miss effect-opaque skips. Learned policies are a **secondary** tool —
not the primary substitute for planning while \(f\) remains cheap and exact.

Claims of Option-SMDP completeness, Bellman optimality of beam, Newton identity of
current code, and “meta-intelligence” require measured multi-time models, explicit
\(\langle\mathcal{I},\pi,\beta\rangle\), and ROM-complete cost libraries before they are
theorems.

## 6. Counter-adversary dialogue (compressed)

**Advocate:** The Gita’s “renounce fruit” is exactly why we use potential shaping and
refuse score farming.

**Prosecutor:** No. Ng preserves the *same* optimal policies under a transform. The
Gita asks the *agent* not to be owned by outcomes. DP agents are defined as fruit
optimizers. At best you have researcher hygiene.

**Advocate:** Bellman’s PoO is the Gita’s “remaining decisions must be right for the
resulting state.”

**Prosecutor:** Shared English silhouette. Bellman’s claim is: suboptimal *tails*
falsify optimality of the whole — a theorem schema. Krishna’s claim is ethical
continuation under metaphysics of Self. Kill the identity.

**Advocate:** Our whistle gap shows that foresight requires something like wisdom
beyond greedy progress.

**Prosecutor:** It shows that **execute-to-observe search over opaque options** beats
myopic hill-climb when effects are unknown. That is planner-class / model
incompleteness. Calling it “wisdom” or “meta-intelligence” outruns the artifact while
second whistles are still re-granted and Bowser is symbolic.

**Advocate:** Then the theory stack is cargo cult.

**Prosecutor:** No. Search-primary under exact \(f\) is well-supported; V6 prior-as-guide
is real; AcquireWhistle_1_3 is real. The wound is **vocabulary inflation** and
**honesty debt**, not the engineering core. Narrow the thesis; climb the knowledge
ladder; stop saying Newton unless the operator identity holds.

**Advocate:** What should we *do* with the Gita after this?

**Prosecutor:** Keep it as a **mirror for the experimenter**: do the hard prescribed
work (ROM-complete options, replay gates); renounce attachment to intermediate fruit
(bench green, val-acc, poetic isomorphism); do not confuse idle philosophy with
action; do not abandon the task because it is unpleasant. Put that in the notes. Do
not put it in the loss function.
