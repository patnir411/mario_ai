## Durable verified facts

- Emulator snapshots and deterministic forward dynamics are the trusted low-level
  substrate; the determinism and snapshot tests are the first verification gate.
- SMB1 beam/coverage/segmented search handles ordinary, pipe, water, and loop-castle
  levels. Canonical manifests are accepted only after independent replay.
- `GameAdapter` supports NES SMB1 plus ROM-gated SML and SMA4 paths. NES and GBA
  emulator dependencies require separate environments because their `pyglet`
  constraints conflict.
- SMA4 1-1 and 1-2, both whistle acquisitions, and real whistle-spend transitions
  have replay-backed local artifacts. This validates the components, not continuous
  composition from a single root.
- The live post-1-2 map route to the real fortress is
  `DOWN,DOWN,LEFT -> (96,96)`. From the declared 1-2 root it retains 1,733 frames
  and ends at exact emulator SHA-256 `9173ec00...f9762`, identically twice.
- Stable-Retro/mGBA savestate bytes are not canonical across load/resnapshot. Raw
  equality is exact continuity; RAM/wrapper/fixed-suffix agreement is only a
  labeled finite restore attestation, never proof of general equivalence.
